# LuxeRoutes ethical backlink plan

## Principles and measurement

Earn links through genuinely useful resources and documented relationships. Never buy undisclosed links, automate unsolicited bulk outreach, exchange links at scale, or describe a link as acquired before it is live. Review referring-domain relevance, linked URL, anchor text, publication date, relationship, and status quarterly. Use descriptive, varied anchors rather than exact-match repetition.

## Suitable publishers and partners

- Official national, regional and municipal tourism organisations in Slovenia, Croatia, Austria and northern Italy.
- Independently operated hotels, glamping sites, wine estates, guides and responsible activity providers actually mentioned in an approved guide.
- European road-trip, architecture, food, wine, wellness and accessible-travel publications with named editors and real editorial standards.
- Motoring clubs, EV route resources, ferry operators and destination transport sites where a logistics guide supplies genuine utility.
- Chambers of tourism, convention bureaux, cultural institutions, protected-area education pages and university hospitality programmes.
- Curated travel-resource directories that review submissions and do not exist primarily to sell links.

## Link-worthy assets to develop

1. A maintained cross-border Alpine driving checklist covering vignettes, environmental zones, seasonal equipment and rental permissions, with official-source citations.
2. A Slovenia–Croatia ferry and border planning map, updated by season.
3. A transparent boutique-hotel evaluation worksheet with printable questions.
4. A shoulder-season calendar comparing climate, closures and crowd patterns.
5. Interviews with named local experts, published only with permission and factual review.
6. An accessible-stay questionnaire that providers can complete and travellers can reuse.

## Guest-post ideas

- “How to design a low-mileage Slovenia road trip” for a responsible-travel publication.
- “Seven questions to ask before booking an Alpine chalet” for a ski or family-travel site.
- “Istria without daily hotel changes” for a Croatia specialist.
- “What boutique actually means in European hospitality” for an independent hotel association.
- “Planning a wine route with a sober transport strategy” for a food-and-wine publisher.

Every pitch should offer original expertise, disclose commercial relationships and permit the editor to use `nofollow` or `sponsored` when appropriate.

## Accommodation and tourism collaboration

Invite providers to fact-check practical details in relevant coverage without promising positive treatment or a link. Offer approved providers the **Featured on LuxeRoutes** badge from `work-with-luxeroutes.html`; record usage only after verifying the live page. Propose co-authored logistics resources to tourism boards, interviews with local guides, or data contributions from ferry and mobility operators. Keep editorial inclusion separate from affiliate or paid terms.

## Workflow

1. Qualify relevance, editorial quality and contact legitimacy.
2. Read the target page and formulate one specific contribution.
3. Send one personalised message; at most one polite follow-up after 10–14 days.
4. Log replies and honour opt-outs. Do not create accounts or publish externally without explicit approval.
5. Verify any live link manually and record its exact URL and disclosure type.
6. Recheck live links and factual accuracy quarterly; update the linked LuxeRoutes asset when rules change.
