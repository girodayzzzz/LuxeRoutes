## Operating rules

- Check each Facebook group or forum’s promotional-link policy before posting; participate only where the answer is genuinely relevant.
- Do not post the same forum answer in multiple threads or send unsolicited direct messages.
- Reply to comments with useful planning information; do not promise prices, availability, reservations or response times.
- Record results in `POSTING-TRACKER.csv`, then reuse only formats that earn meaningful saves, clicks or inquiries.
- Any direct Partner Offers post must retain the affiliate disclosure provided in the marketing pack.
