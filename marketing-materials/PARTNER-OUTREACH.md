## Information and permissions required before publication

- Legal business name, trading name, registration details and primary contact.
- Official website, booking contact and the exact booking or inquiry process.
- Accurate address, map location, service area and seasonal operating dates.
- Current description, inclusions, exclusions, capacity and accessibility information.
- A statement confirming that the provider controls and confirms prices and availability.
- Booking, payment, deposit, cancellation, refund and customer-support terms.
- Applicable licenses, insurance or safety requirements where relevant; LuxeRoutes will not imply independent certification.
- Written permission to publish the business name, approved description, contact details and links.
- Written confirmation that the provider owns or has sufficient rights to every supplied photograph, video, logo and testimonial, including permission for LuxeRoutes to crop or resize media for the agreed channels.
- Image credits, usage duration, territories and any restrictions.
- Written approval of the final factual copy and media selection before publication.
- Written agreement on inquiry attribution, data handling and who responds to travelers.
- If a commission is agreed: calculation basis, qualifying event, cancellations, reporting, invoicing, taxes and payment timing.
- A contact and process for corrections, seasonal updates or removal requests.

LuxeRoutes should retain the permission record and approved copy. No private provider should be published until all necessary written permissions are complete.
