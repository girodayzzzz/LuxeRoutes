# LuxeRoutes social assets

Six original, editable SVG graphics use only vector shapes and the LuxeRoutes wordmark treatment; they do not depend on third-party photography. SVG was selected because no local SVG-to-PNG renderer is installed. Export at the intrinsic dimensions shown below without cropping.

- Instagram/Facebook: `instagram-*.svg` — 1080 × 1350
- Stories: `story-*.svg` — 1080 × 1920
- Pinterest: `pinterest-*.svg` — 1000 × 1500

Keep all text within the inset keyline. Before posting, open the target URL, check the copy and confirm that platform compression preserves readability.
