Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 19 September 2026

How LuxeRoutes is funded

LuxeRoutes publishes independent travel inspiration and guides. Some links lead to external booking or comparison providers and are affiliate links. If you follow one of those links and complete a qualifying transaction, LuxeRoutes may receive a commission. This does not add a fee to the price charged by the provider.

How affiliate links are identified

Articles include a disclosure before their first affiliate link. Sponsored external links use clear calls to action and technical link attributes. A link may redirect through an affiliate network before reaching the named provider.

External bookings

LuxeRoutes does not own the accommodation, vehicle, flight or activity inventory shown by external providers. Bookings, payments, amendments, cancellations, prices and availability are handled by the provider under its own terms and privacy policy. “Check current prices and availability” means that all live commercial information must be verified on the provider’s website.

Editorial independence

Compensation may influence which commercially available links can be included, but it does not justify fabricated experiences, ratings or guarantees. A destination or stay idea is travel inspiration unless a real provider is clearly identified. Inclusion does not mean that LuxeRoutes has personally inspected a property.

Your choice

You are not required to use an affiliate link. You can visit a provider independently. Compare terms, total price, cancellation conditions and suitability before booking.
