Slovenia is compact enough for an ambitious week, yet varied enough that rushing defeats the point. This circular itinerary links Ljubljana, the Julian Alps, the Soča Valley and wine country with realistic drives and two-night pauses.

At a glance

Recommended length: 7 days. Best period: Late May–June or September; use July–August only if you book early. Getting around: Collect a car after Ljubljana; confirm seasonal opening of Vršič Pass before departure. The strongest plan gives each important base enough time and keeps arrival days deliberately light.

Day-by-day itinerary

Distances and driving times for The Perfect 7-Day Luxury Road Trip Through Slovenia are approximate and exclude long stops, congestion and weather delays. Check current road conditions before setting out.

Day 1: Ljubljana to Ljubljana

Approximate distance
0–15 km
Driving time
up to 30 minutes
Main stops
Riverside old town and castle viewpoints
Realistic plan
Keep arrival gentle: walk the centre and reserve one dinner.
Stay style
Central boutique hotel
Practical tip
Collect a rental car tomorrow; city parking is unnecessary today.

Day 2: Ljubljana to Bled

Approximate distance
about 55 km
Driving time
about 45–60 minutes
Main stops
Radovljica and Lake Bled
Realistic plan
Visit Radovljica, check in, then circle part of the lake late afternoon.
Stay style
Quiet hotel or guesthouse outside the busiest shore
Practical tip
Reserve legal parking with the accommodation.

Day 3: Bled to Bohinj and Bled

Approximate distance
about 60–80 km return
Driving time
about 1.5–2 hours total
Main stops
Lake Bohinj, Ribčev Laz and an optional cable-car area
Realistic plan
Choose one walk and one lake activity; do not combine every gorge and summit.
Stay style
Second night near Bled
Practical tip
Check mountain weather before leaving.

Day 4: Bled to Soča Valley

Approximate distance
about 85–110 km
Driving time
about 2–3 hours
Main stops
Kranjska Gora, Vršič Pass or the all-weather Predel alternative
Realistic plan
Treat the mountain crossing as the day’s main experience.
Stay style
Mountain lodge or small hotel near Bovec/Kobarid
Practical tip
Vršič is seasonal; verify official road conditions.

Day 5: Soča Valley to Soča Valley

Approximate distance
about 25–50 km
Driving time
under 1.5 hours
Main stops
Kobarid museum, riverside paths and Tolmin area
Realistic plan
Select a guided activity suited to conditions or a cultural day.
Stay style
Second valley night
Practical tip
River activities require a qualified local operator.

Day 6: Soča Valley to Goriška Brda

Approximate distance
about 55–75 km
Driving time
about 1.25–1.75 hours
Main stops
Kanal, Šmartno and vineyard landscapes
Realistic plan
Arrive for lunch, take one arranged tasting and leave the evening free.
Stay style
Wine-estate room
Practical tip
Arrange a driver if tasting alcohol.

Day 7: Goriška Brda to Ljubljana

Approximate distance
about 140 km
Driving time
about 2 hours
Main stops
Vipava old town or Predjama area
Realistic plan
Choose a single stop before returning the car in Ljubljana.
Stay style
Airport or city hotel if departing next day
Practical tip
Leave extra time for rental return and fuel.

Where to stay

Boutique city hotel, lakeside guesthouse, mountain lodge and wine-estate room. Compare the exact room, cancellation policy, taxes, parking and meal arrangements rather than relying on a category label. Boutique does not automatically mean quiet, accessible or fully serviced. Request written confirmation for any essential feature, especially air conditioning, step-free access, private outdoor space or electric-vehicle charging.
