Places and decisions that shape this trip

Understand the structure

A safari tent, timber cabin and geodesic dome behave differently in wind, heat and rain. Ask whether the unit is insulated, lockable and fully enclosed, and whether the bathroom is private, shared or reached outdoors.

Check summer comfort

Adriatic sites can remain hot after sunset. Confirm shade, ventilation and genuine air conditioning rather than relying on styled photographs. Inland and Alpine sites need a clear wet-weather plan and heating outside midsummer.

Privacy is site-specific

A private deck does not mean a private setting. Request a site plan or approximate spacing, ask where paths and communal facilities run, and check whether events or quiet hours affect the atmosphere.

Match service to expectations

Some camps deliver breakfast baskets and daily housekeeping; others are intentionally self-catering. Verify linen changes, cooking equipment, parking distance and late-arrival arrangements before comparing rates.

Where to stay

Insulated safari tent, treehouse, cabin or geodesic dome with a private bathroom. Compare the exact room, cancellation policy, taxes, parking and meal arrangements rather than relying on a category label. Boutique does not automatically mean quiet, accessible or fully serviced. Request written confirmation for any essential feature, especially air conditioning, step-free access, private outdoor space or electric-vehicle charging.

Transport and access details

A car is usually necessary; verify the final access road, parking and luggage transfer. Before paying, check the provider’s fuel policy, deposit, excess, permitted countries and roadside assistance. Urban hotels may sit inside restricted traffic zones. For trains and ferries, allow a buffer around flight arrivals and confirm whether reservations are mandatory.

A practical final checklist

  • Confirm passport, driving licence, insurance and cross-border requirements.
  • Save bookings, maps and emergency details offline.
  • Ask accommodation providers about arrival windows and local access.
  • Keep one flexible half-day for weather or an unplanned discovery.
  • Review cancellation terms and pay only through trusted provider channels.
