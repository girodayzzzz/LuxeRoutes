Before you inquire

Questions about personalized route research

Is the Travel Research Brief a confirmed itinerary or package holiday?

No. It is informational research to support your own planning. It does not confirm accommodation, transport, availability or a package.

Who handles bookings and payments?

Any booking, payment, amendment or cancellation is completed with the relevant external provider under that provider’s terms.

What should I include in my request?

Share your dates or date range, group size, preferred pace, regions, approximate budget guidance and any essential stay or transport requirements.
